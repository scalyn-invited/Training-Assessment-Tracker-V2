Ownership and rejection
  • Primary owns imported people/groups; local cohorts and coordinator assignments remain app-owned. Training owns evidence and approval history.
  • Duplicate/stale events rejected; conflict queue handles incompatible versions. Suspension/tombstones revoke access and invalidate cache; freshness target five minutes.
Promotion is explicit
  • Real local-only people train and appear in authorised local reports; no primary query/export. Synthetic records never cross production report, mail or integration boundaries.
  • Promotion timeout → reconcile original key. Ack plus verified mapping required before linked. Duplicate review returns to correction; history export is a separate action.
Reliable and private
  • Outbox shares business transaction. Reconcile stable pages; cursor advances only after persistence. Every outbound retry rechecks linked/production/non-synthetic policy.
  • Private evidence: scan/quarantine, authorised download, no unrestricted URLs. Retention/erasure covers files, caches, exports and traces; holds and deletion ledger survive restore.
