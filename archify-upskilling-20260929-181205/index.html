Design summary

Shared OIDC + mandatory MFA; application role, group, ownership and environment checks. Primary platform owns imported people/groups; training owns evidence and immutable approvals. Real local-only learners stay in local reports. AI creates drafts and provisional results, with coordinator approval before official publication.

Approve the full baseline before activation. Keep current and next weeks ready. Week N evidence normally changes N+2; cutoff is 17:00 two business days before target start, or immediate lock on activity. Late reviews retain baseline.
