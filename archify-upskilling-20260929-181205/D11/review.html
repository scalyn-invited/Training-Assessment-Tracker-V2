Measure what and why
  • Every KPI records owner, what/why, baseline/date, target, formula, unit/direction, evidence, cadence, sample minimum and version.
  • Keep participation, demonstrated competence and workplace outcome separate. Weighted criterion percentages sum with weights = 1; incomparable scales need explicit mapping.
Honest interpretation
  • Insufficient samples or missing baseline → insufficient_evidence, never zero. Effective-dated target changes never rewrite old snapshots.
  • Local reports include genuine local-only members; primary API excludes local-only/pending/synthetic. Synthetic data stays out of all production totals.
Useful suggestions and privacy
  • One to three feasible suggestions fit remaining time. Current advice adds no required work; required changes pass coordinator gate and D10 freeze policy.
  • Preserve provenance and calculation versions. Retention/holds/erasure extend to derived snapshots and exports. No public ranking or unsupported employment decisions.
